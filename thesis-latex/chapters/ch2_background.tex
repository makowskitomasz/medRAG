\label{sec:lm_to_rag}

% Placeholder paragraph:
Large language models (LLMs) excel at fluent text generation and general-purpose
reasoning, but suffer from two fundamental limitations in knowledge-intensive
applications: (1)~their parametric knowledge is frozen at training time, making
them unreliable for domains that evolve rapidly; and (2)~they are prone to
\emph{hallucination} --- the generation of plausible-sounding but factually
incorrect statements~\cite{ji2023hallucination}.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation, introduced by Lewis~\etal~\cite{lewis2020rag},
addresses both issues by augmenting the generation process with dynamically
retrieved documents, grounding each response in citable source material.

\section{RAG Architecture Families}
\label{sec:rag_families}

The literature on RAG has expanded rapidly since 2020.
Table~\ref{tab:rag_families} provides an overview of the eight architecture families
surveyed in this chapter, ordered chronologically and by complexity.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Overview of surveyed RAG architecture families.}
  \label{tab:rag_families}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lllX@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Family} & \textbf{Year} & \textbf{Key paper} & \textbf{Core idea} \\
    \midrule
    Vanilla RAG & 2020 & Lewis et al.~\cite{lewis2020rag}
      & Single-pass retrieve-then-generate \\
    HyDE / Query Rewriting & 2022 & Gao et al.~\cite{gao2022hyde}
      & Embed hypothetical answer to improve retrieval alignment \\
    Self-RAG & 2023 & Asai et al.~\cite{asai2023selfrag}
      & Reflection tokens for adaptive retrieval and self-critique \\
    Corrective RAG & 2024 & Yan et al.~\cite{yan2024crag}
      & Correctness evaluation with web-search fallback \\
    Graph / Hierarchical RAG & 2024 & Edge et al.~\cite{edge2024graphrag}; Sarthi et al.~\cite{sarthi2024raptor}
      & Knowledge graphs and hierarchical tree summaries \\
    Iterative Multi-hop RAG & 2025 & Wang et al.~\cite{wang2025chain}
      & Multi-step evidence chaining across retrieval calls \\
    Agentic / Multi-Agent RAG & 2025 & Singh et al.~\cite{singh2025agenticrag}
      & Planner--executor agents, tool-augmented retrieval \\
    \rarerag{} (this work) & 2026 & ---
      & Routing + hybrid retrieval + set-wise selection + grounding verifier \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
